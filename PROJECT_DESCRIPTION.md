## What This Project Does

This is an **AI-powered forecasting system** that predicts steel prices (specifically UAE Rebar import prices) for tomorrow, next week, and next month. It helps businesses make smarter procurement decisions, save money, and plan budgets more accurately.

Think of it as a "weather forecast" but for steel prices - instead of predicting rain or sunshine, it predicts whether steel prices will go up or down, and by how much.

---

## Why This Matters

### The Problem:
Steel prices change daily based on dozens of factors:
- Raw material costs (iron ore, coal, scrap metal)
- Energy prices (oil, gas)
- Shipping costs
- Global economic trends
- Currency exchange rates

Without accurate forecasting, companies either:
- **Buy too early** and waste money when prices drop
- **Buy too late** and pay premium prices
- **Budget incorrectly** for projects and lose profit margins

### The Solution:
Our system analyzes 17 different market indicators and provides:
- **Daily price forecasts** with 99.84% accuracy
- **Confidence ranges** (how certain the prediction is)
- **Multi-timeframe predictions** (1-day, 7-day, 30-day ahead)
- **Interactive dashboard** anyone can use (no technical skills needed)
- **Cost savings calculator** showing ROI
